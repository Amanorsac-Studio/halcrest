About us

A Charlottesville small business built on dependable delivery.

Halcrest Group LLC is a Virginia-based small business in Charlottesville that provides technology and creative services to government agencies, businesses and organizations. We design and build custom software, websites and mobile apps, and provide IT support. We also offer professional audio, video and music production, including live sound and audiovisual setup for events.

Halcrest serves clients through direct contracts and public bids, with a focus on reliable delivery at fair prices. We also take on general services work, so clients can bring a wide range of needs to one responsive partner.
